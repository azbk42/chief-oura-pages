Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Chief Personal Monitoring is a private, local-first application used by its owner for personal performance tracking.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes Oura access using OAuth, Chief may import available sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, stress and other data within the scopes granted to the app. Chief also processes manually entered journals and stores the OAuth credentials required for the connection.

Purpose and use

Data are used to display daily history and monitor the owner's recovery, sleep, activity and habits. Data are not sold or used for advertising. AI-assisted analysis, when enabled by the owner, may send selected context to the configured third-party AI provider; it is not required to view imported Oura records.

Storage

Oura records and OAuth tokens are stored in the local Chief data vault on the owner's device. This publicly hosted informational website does not access or store Oura records. The security of local records depends on the owner's device and backup practices.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Records are retained locally until the owner deletes them or the local vault. Revoking Oura authorization prevents future access but does not necessarily delete records already imported or stored in backups. The owner can delete local records using the application or its data-storage tools.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. For inquiries, email mauduit.pro@outlook.com.
